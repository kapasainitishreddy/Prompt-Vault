## Website Design

### Type & visual hierarchy

#### [Editorial first-fold](../docs/concepts.html#web-editorial-first-fold)

**User problem:** Lead with a clear thesis and one real piece of visual evidence.

**Use when:** Narrative products, studios, long-form brands.

**Avoid when:** A product requiring immediate filtering or account access.

**Recommended skills:** typography, content architecture, responsive CSS. **Evaluation:** Can readers locate the purpose and next action without scanning the entire page?

**Sources:** [Nielsen Norman Group: Recognition Rather Than Recall](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/recognition-and-recall/); [Sweller (1988): Cognitive load during problem solving](https://doi.org/10.1207/s15516709cog1202_4); [W3C: Clear and Understandable Content](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG2/supplemental/objectives/o3-clear-content/); [Hassenzahl (2004): Interplay of Beauty, Goodness and Usability](https://doi.org/10.1207/s15327051hci1904_2).

#### [Asymmetric grid](../docs/concepts.html#web-asymmetric-grid)

**User problem:** Create distinct visual weight while retaining a predictable reading path.

**Use when:** Case studies or creative direction with contrasting content.

**Avoid when:** A dense dashboard where asymmetric data ordering confuses comparisons.

**Recommended skills:** typography, content architecture, responsive CSS. **Evaluation:** Can readers locate the purpose and next action without scanning the entire page?

**Sources:** [Nielsen Norman Group: Recognition Rather Than Recall](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/recognition-and-recall/); [Sweller (1988): Cognitive load during problem solving](https://doi.org/10.1207/s15516709cog1202_4); [W3C: Clear and Understandable Content](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG2/supplemental/objectives/o3-clear-content/).

#### [Oversized typographic hierarchy](../docs/concepts.html#web-oversized-typographic-hierarchy)

**User problem:** Let type carry the message with readable follow-up content.

**Use when:** Strong concise product positioning and art-directed portfolios.

**Avoid when:** Long localized headings or tiny supporting copy.

**Recommended skills:** typography, content architecture, responsive CSS. **Evaluation:** Can readers locate the purpose and next action without scanning the entire page?

**Sources:** [Nielsen Norman Group: Recognition Rather Than Recall](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/recognition-and-recall/); [Sweller (1988): Cognitive load during problem solving](https://doi.org/10.1207/s15516709cog1202_4); [W3C: Clear and Understandable Content](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG2/supplemental/objectives/o3-clear-content/).

#### [Split-screen narrative](../docs/concepts.html#web-split-screen-narrative)

#### [Minimalist utility](../docs/concepts.html#web-minimalist-utility)

**Use when:** Focused tools, contact pages, essential settings.

**Recommended skills:** typography, content architecture, responsive CSS. **Evaluation:** Can readers locate the purpose and next action without scanning the entire page?

**Sources:** [Nielsen Norman Group: Recognition Rather Than Recall](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/recognition-and-recall/); [Sweller (1988): Cognitive load during problem solving](https://doi.org/10.1207/s15516709cog1202_4); [W3C: Clear and Understandable Content](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG2/supplemental/objectives/o3-clear-content/).

#### [Responsive art-directed type](../docs/concepts.html#web-responsive-art-directed-type)

**User problem:** Preserve typographic personality without clipping on small screens.

**Use when:** Display-heavy campaigns, multilingual marketing pages.

**Avoid when:** Fonts or rigid line breaks cause overlap or assistive-tech loss.

**Recommended skills:** typography, content architecture, responsive CSS. **Evaluation:** Can readers locate the purpose and next action without scanning the entire page?

**Sources:** [Nielsen Norman Group: Recognition Rather Than Recall](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/recognition-and-recall/); [Sweller (1988): Cognitive load during problem solving](https://doi.org/10.1207/s15516709cog1202_4); [W3C: Clear and Understandable Content](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG2/supplemental/objectives/o3-clear-content/).

#### [Mixed-media editorial rhythm](../docs/concepts.html#web-mixed-media-editorial-rhythm)

**User problem:** Sequence images, captions, quotations and text by importance.

**Use when:** Process stories, creator showcases and magazines.

**Avoid when:** Media dominates before users can find the meaning.

### Navigation & discoverability

#### [Task-first navigation](../docs/concepts.html#web-task-first-navigation)

**User problem:** Organize labels around user goals rather than internal departments.

**Use when:** Companies with mixed visitor intent.

**Avoid when:** Internal jargon forces visitors to guess categories.

**Recommended skills:** information architecture, keyboard interactions, wayfinding. **Evaluation:** Can users get from entry to a named destination and return without confusion?

**Sources:** [Nielsen Norman Group: Recognition Rather Than Recall](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/recognition-and-recall/); [W3C: ARIA Authoring Practices patterns](https://www.w3.org/WAI/ARIA/apg/patterns/); [Hyman (1953): Stimulus information and reaction time](https://doi.org/10.1037/h0056940).

#### [Adaptive desktop navigation](../docs/concepts.html#web-adaptive-desktop-navigation)

**User problem:** Keep a stable set of destinations as available width changes.

**Use when:** Cross-device corporate or product sites.

**Avoid when:** A mobile menu merely hides unreachable links.

**Recommended skills:** information architecture, keyboard interactions, wayfinding. **Evaluation:** Can users get from entry to a named destination and return without confusion?

**Sources:** [Nielsen Norman Group: Recognition Rather Than Recall](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/recognition-and-recall/); [W3C: ARIA Authoring Practices patterns](https://www.w3.org/WAI/ARIA/apg/patterns/); [Hyman (1953): Stimulus information and reaction time](https://doi.org/10.1037/h0056940).

#### [Progressive navigation disclosure](../docs/concepts.html#web-progressive-navigation-disclosure)

**User problem:** Offer a small set of visible routes and show the rest on demand.

**Use when:** Layered documentation and expansive sites.

**Avoid when:** Primary actions are hidden behind unexplained menus.

**Recommended skills:** information architecture, keyboard interactions, wayfinding. **Evaluation:** Can users get from entry to a named destination and return without confusion?

**Sources:** [Nielsen Norman Group: Recognition Rather Than Recall](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/recognition-and-recall/); [W3C: ARIA Authoring Practices patterns](https://www.w3.org/WAI/ARIA/apg/patterns/); [Hyman (1953): Stimulus information and reaction time](https://doi.org/10.1037/h0056940).

#### [Accessible mega-menu](../docs/concepts.html#web-accessible-mega-menu)

**User problem:** Group many destinations with clear headings and keyboard escape.

**Use when:** Genuine high-volume product or service catalogs.

**Avoid when:** A small site with six links wastes space.

**Recommended skills:** information architecture, keyboard interactions, wayfinding. **Evaluation:** Can users get from entry to a named destination and return without confusion?

**Sources:** [Nielsen Norman Group: Recognition Rather Than Recall](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/recognition-and-recall/); [W3C: ARIA Authoring Practices patterns](https://www.w3.org/WAI/ARIA/apg/patterns/); [Hyman (1953): Stimulus information and reaction time](https://doi.org/10.1037/h0056940).

#### [Breadcrumb hierarchy](../docs/concepts.html#web-breadcrumb-hierarchy)

**User problem:** Show parent context and a short return path.

**Use when:** Deep articles, taxonomy pages and product collections.

**Avoid when:** Flat sites with no meaningful parent chain.

**Recommended skills:** information architecture, keyboard interactions, wayfinding. **Evaluation:** Can users get from entry to a named destination and return without confusion?

**Sources:** [Nielsen Norman Group: Recognition Rather Than Recall](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/recognition-and-recall/); [W3C: ARIA Authoring Practices patterns](https://www.w3.org/WAI/ARIA/apg/patterns/); [Hyman (1953): Stimulus information and reaction time](https://doi.org/10.1037/h0056940).

#### [Persistent contextual subnav](../docs/concepts.html#web-persistent-contextual-subnav)

**User problem:** Keep local sections reachable as an article or case study grows.

**Use when:** Long scientific or technical references.

**Avoid when:** Sticky navigation consumes small screens.

**Recommended skills:** information architecture, keyboard interactions, wayfinding. **Evaluation:** Can users get from entry to a named destination and return without confusion?

**Sources:** [Nielsen Norman Group: Recognition Rather Than Recall](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/recognition-and-recall/); [W3C: ARIA Authoring Practices patterns](https://www.w3.org/WAI/ARIA/apg/patterns/); [Hyman (1953): Stimulus information and reaction time](https://doi.org/10.1037/h0056940).

#### [Search-first orientation](../docs/concepts.html#web-search-first-orientation)

**User problem:** Start with a discoverable query when users know what they want.

**Use when:** Documentation, component catalogs and directories.

**Avoid when:** Search is unreliable and browsing paths are omitted.

**Recommended skills:** information architecture, keyboard interactions, wayfinding. **Evaluation:** Can users get from entry to a named destination and return without confusion?

**Sources:** [Nielsen Norman Group: Recognition Rather Than Recall](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/recognition-and-recall/); [W3C: ARIA Authoring Practices patterns](https://www.w3.org/WAI/ARIA/apg/patterns/); [Hyman (1953): Stimulus information and reaction time](https://doi.org/10.1037/h0056940).

#### [Faceted filtering](../docs/concepts.html#web-faceted-filtering)

**User problem:** Use meaningful attributes, active chips, counts and reset.

**Use when:** Large product lists with real metadata.

**Avoid when:** Filtering small result sets adds cost and complexity.

**Recommended skills:** information architecture, keyboard interactions, wayfinding. **Evaluation:** Can users get from entry to a named destination and return without confusion?

**Sources:** [Nielsen Norman Group: Recognition Rather Than Recall](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/recognition-and-recall/); [W3C: ARIA Authoring Practices patterns](https://www.w3.org/WAI/ARIA/apg/patterns/); [Hyman (1953): Stimulus information and reaction time](https://doi.org/10.1037/h0056940).

#### [Keyboard command palette](../docs/concepts.html#web-keyboard-command-palette)

**User problem:** Give expert users accelerated access to actions and pages.

**Use when:** Complex authenticated web apps.

**Avoid when:** It is the only path to fundamental navigation.

**Recommended skills:** information architecture, keyboard interactions, wayfinding. **Evaluation:** Can users get from entry to a named destination and return without confusion?

#### [Helpful empty search](../docs/concepts.html#web-helpful-empty-search)

**User problem:** Preserve query and propose nearby categories or corrective terms.

**Use when:** Catalog search with varied terminology.

**Avoid when:** The system fabricates products or silently changes scope.

**Recommended skills:** information architecture, keyboard interactions, wayfinding. **Evaluation:** Can users get from entry to a named destination and return without confusion?

**Sources:** [Nielsen Norman Group: Recognition Rather Than Recall](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/recognition-and-recall/); [W3C: ARIA Authoring Practices patterns](https://www.w3.org/WAI/ARIA/apg/patterns/); [Hyman (1953): Stimulus information and reaction time](https://doi.org/10.1037/h0056940).

### Product storytelling & conversion

#### [Outcome-led hero](../docs/concepts.html#web-outcome-led-hero)

**User problem:** State who benefits, what changes and the supported action.

**Use when:** New SaaS or focused mobile product launch.

**Avoid when:** Claims rely on imaginary results.

**Recommended skills:** product positioning, conversion copy, storyboarding. **Evaluation:** Can a visitor explain the product's benefit and identify the next real step?

**Sources:** [U.S. Web Design System: Design Principles](https://designsystem.digital.gov/design-principles/); [Nielsen Norman Group: 10 Usability Heuristics](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/ten-usability-heuristics/); [W3C: WCAG 2.2](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/).

#### [Problem-to-solution story](../docs/concepts.html#web-problem-to-solution-story)

**User problem:** Show a relatable problem before the demonstrated solution.

**Use when:** Products introducing an unfamiliar workflow.

**Avoid when:** An invented pain point replaces customer research.

**Recommended skills:** product positioning, conversion copy, storyboarding. **Evaluation:** Can a visitor explain the product's benefit and identify the next real step?

**Sources:** [U.S. Web Design System: Design Principles](https://designsystem.digital.gov/design-principles/); [Nielsen Norman Group: 10 Usability Heuristics](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/ten-usability-heuristics/); [W3C: WCAG 2.2](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/).

#### [Real-product interface showcase](../docs/concepts.html#web-real-product-interface-showcase)

**User problem:** Show the actual system in its authentic state.

**Use when:** Apps and developer tools whose workflow is a selling point.

**Avoid when:** Mock screens are presented as real product features.

**Recommended skills:** product positioning, conversion copy, storyboarding. **Evaluation:** Can a visitor explain the product's benefit and identify the next real step?

**Sources:** [U.S. Web Design System: Design Principles](https://designsystem.digital.gov/design-principles/); [Nielsen Norman Group: 10 Usability Heuristics](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/ten-usability-heuristics/); [W3C: WCAG 2.2](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/).

#### [Interactive product demo](../docs/concepts.html#web-interactive-product-demo)

#### [Feature-to-outcome mapping](../docs/concepts.html#web-feature-to-outcome-mapping)

**User problem:** Connect capability, mechanism and human outcome without repetitive cards.

**Use when:** Products with multiple meaningful capabilities.

**Avoid when:** A list of buzzwords substitutes for a demonstration.

**Recommended skills:** product positioning, conversion copy, storyboarding. **Evaluation:** Can a visitor explain the product's benefit and identify the next real step?

**Sources:** [U.S. Web Design System: Design Principles](https://designsystem.digital.gov/design-principles/); [Nielsen Norman Group: 10 Usability Heuristics](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/ten-usability-heuristics/); [W3C: WCAG 2.2](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/).

### Commerce & marketplaces

#### [Search autocomplete suggestions](../docs/concepts.html#web-search-autocomplete-suggestions)

**User problem:** Offer transparent completions that respect typed wording.

**Use when:** Large inventories with synonym-rich queries.

**Avoid when:** Suggestions override, mislead or cannot be keyboard selected.

**Recommended skills:** product listing UX, commerce forms, data clarity. **Evaluation:** Can users find, compare and purchase without hidden fees or mistakes?

**Sources:** [Baymard Institute: Ecommerce Search UX](https://baymard.com/research/ecommerce-search); [Baymard Institute: Product Lists & Filtering UX](https://baymard.com/research/ecommerce-product-lists); [Mathur et al. (2019): Dark Patterns at Scale](https://doi.org/10.1145/3359183).

**Avoid when:** Filter options are fabricated or hide the result count.

**Recommended skills:** product listing UX, commerce forms, data clarity. **Evaluation:** Can users find, compare and purchase without hidden fees or mistakes?
