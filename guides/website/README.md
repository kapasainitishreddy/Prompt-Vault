# Website Design Academy: 20 practical page architectures

Only the **website track**. Site patterns are organized by visitor job rather than obligatory blocks. Open a blueprint to learn exact section placement, reasons, three art directions, skills, performance, AI and 3D decision, relevant source IDs, and a complete coding-agent prompt.

1. [Engineering portfolio](engineer-portfolio.md) — Assess engineering judgment and open a real project.
2. [Designer / creative portfolio](designer-portfolio.md) — Understand visual craft, process and real delivered work.
3. [Startup company site](startup-company.md) — Find out what the company does, why trust it and how to contact.
4. [Enterprise corporate site](enterprise-company.md) — Verify services, governance, credibility, support and operations.
5. [SaaS product landing page](saas-marketing.md) — Understand the product, try a genuine workflow and see terms.
6. [B2B platform / integration site](b2b-platform.md) — Validate integration, risk, documentation and support.
7. [Consumer digital product site](consumer-product.md) — Experience the product benefit and understand pricing/privacy.
8. [Ecommerce product detail](ecommerce-pdp.md) — Judge fit, variant, total cost, delivery and return conditions.
9. [Marketplace / store catalog](ecommerce-catalog.md) — Search, narrow, compare and navigate back without losing filters.
10. [Creative / development agency](creative-agency.md) — See expertise, specific outcomes, process and contact.
11. [Publication / book editorial](editorial-publishing.md) — Find a work, read a real excerpt and subscribe or purchase.
12. [Learning / course website](education-course.md) — Judge outcomes, curriculum level, format and purchase/registration.
13. [Nonprofit / mission site](nonprofit-cause.md) — Understand mission, impact evidence, donation and participation.
14. [Local service business](local-business.md) — Find service area, availability, trust signals and booking.
15. [Conference / event registration](conference-event.md) — Read accurate schedule, place, speakers and register.
16. [Developer documentation / API portal](developer-docs.md) — Find correct version, authentication, code example and troubleshoot.
17. [Multi-product company and subdomain hub](brand-subdomains.md) — Recognize destinations, ownership, account boundaries and return routes.
18. [AI-first product website](ai-native-marketing.md) — Understand data use, what AI can do, reliability and pricing.
19. [Travel / hotel booking](travel-hospitality.md) — Understand rooms, total cost, location and change policy.
20. [Creator / entertainment channel](media-creator.md) — Watch or read a real sample and find the next release.
