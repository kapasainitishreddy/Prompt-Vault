# App Design Academy — 20 cross-platform app blueprints

**Two platforms, one consistent product, platform-appropriate behavior.** The app track is entirely separate from website marketing design. Choose one app job and follow it through navigation, all meaningful states, reliability, accessibility and honest monetization.

1. [Task and to-do app](tasks.md) — Capture, prioritize, finish and undo actions without losing work
2. [Calendar and scheduling](calendar.md) — See commitments, resolve conflicts and reschedule across time zones
3. [Privacy-first journal](journal.md) — Write without setup, save privately, search and export entries
4. [Habits and routines](habits.md) — Plan realistic routines and recover after missing sessions
5. [Workout tracker](fitness.md) — Find exercises, log a session, resume interrupted tracking
6. [Education and language learning](learning.md) — Find level, practice, get accurate feedback and revisit a lesson
7. [Ebook and reading library](reader.md) — Find a title, read, bookmark and resume offline
8. [Creative editor and design canvas](creator.md) — Import, edit, preview, export and recover failed work
9. [Camera and photo editor](camera.md) — Capture, review, edit and export with controlled permissions
10. [Music and video streaming](streaming.md) — Discover media, play and resume, handle downloads/expiry
11. [Social/community network](community.md) — Find relevant communities, post, manage privacy and harm reporting
12. [Messaging and collaboration](messaging.md) — Find a thread, communicate, see delivery state and retry failures
13. [Shopping and marketplace](shopping.md) — Search inventory, compare, buy accurately and inspect order
14. [Travel and booking](travel.md) — Choose dates/inventory, see total cost, book, then amend
15. [Personal finance viewer](finance.md) — See balances, provenance, pending activity and data controls
16. [Meditation and calm practice](wellness.md) — Find a fitting session, complete it, optionally reflect
17. [AI work assistant](ai-copilot.md) — Give a task, review cited evidence, approve actions and recover failure
18. [Offline knowledge base](offline-notes.md) — Capture without network, merge and search safely
19. [Business operations dashboard](b2b-dashboard.md) — Detect anomaly, verify source, act, audit result
20. [Game companion and collectibles](game-companion.md) — Manage current game state, coordinate tasks and preserve progress

Guides: [Native AI](AI-INTEGRATION.md), [3D and motion](MOTION-AND-3D.md), [App Store / Play Store](STORE-RELEASE.md), [Revenue/retention research](REVENUE-AND-RETENTION.md), [UX screens and states](UX-PATTERNS.md). [Evidence](../../research/EVIDENCE-ATLAS.md).

**Important:** these pages are reusable prompt/implementation specs, *not* shipping iOS/Android apps. Every listing must show real app screenshots, permissions, entitlements and device tests.
