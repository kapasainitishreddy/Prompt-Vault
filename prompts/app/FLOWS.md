# App UI/UX flow prompts (32)

**Distinct from website prompts.** These prompts build **task flows, transitions, recovery, accessibility and native/platform-correct behavior**. Each is self-contained with six fill-in inputs and a bounded four-round implementation/testing loop.

- [Onboarding and first run](flows/onboarding.md) — Get users to first meaningful value without forcing irrelevant setup.
- [Sign in and account recovery](flows/authentication.md) — Let users access a real account securely and recover it.
- [Home and task hub](flows/home.md) — Show what matters now, how to act, and current state.
- [App navigation](flows/navigation.md) — Make moving between important jobs reliable and platform-consistent.
- [Search and discovery](flows/search.md) — Find one item precisely with clear matching and recovery.
- [Filters and sorting](flows/filters.md) — Narrow or reorganize results without losing user context.
- [Lists and activity feeds](flows/list-feed.md) — Scan, group and act on repeated records efficiently.
- [Detail screen](flows/detail.md) — Help someone interpret one object and make its next decision.
- [Create and edit](flows/create-edit.md) — Let users produce and save valid work without loss.
- [Multi-step forms](flows/multi-step-form.md) — Collect complex input without overwhelming or losing progress.
- [Tasks and to-dos](flows/task-management.md) — Capture, prioritize, finish and undo without distractions.
- [Calendar and scheduling](flows/calendar.md) — See time truthfully and resolve collisions.
- [Analytics and overview](flows/dashboard-data.md) — Turn up-to-date product data into an informed action.
- [Notifications and inbox](flows/notifications.md) — Prioritize real updates, control interruptions and take action.
- [Messaging and conversation](flows/chat.md) — Read/write messages without ambiguity or lost text.
- [Media collection](flows/media-library.md) — Find, inspect, organize and act on real media.
- [Capture and upload](flows/upload-capture.md) — Add files or recorded content with clear safety, control and feedback.
- [In-app purchase or booking checkout](flows/checkout.md) — Commit money with exact price and reliable receipt.
- [Premium plan decision](flows/paywall.md) — Help users make informed subscription choice without coercion.
- [User profile](flows/profile.md) — Present identity, control and privacy without fantasy data.
- [Settings and data control](flows/settings-privacy.md) — Help users change preferences, manage account and understand privacy.
- [Permission request and denial](flows/permissions.md) — Ask for OS capabilities at appropriate moments and recover gracefully.
- [Offline work and sync recovery](flows/offline-sync.md) — Keep important data durable and communicate sync truth.
- [Useful empty states](flows/empty-states.md) — Explain why there is no data and how to start without shame.
- [Errors and recovery](flows/errors-recovery.md) — Turn failure into a practical and honest next step.
- [Undo and destructive actions](flows/undo-confirmation.md) — Make consequential actions reversible or meaningfully confirmed.
- [Accessibility pass](flows/accessibility.md) — Allow users with assistive tech or motor/vision differences to finish tasks.
- [Multilingual and RTL](flows/localization-rtl.md) — Ensure language and reading direction work in the whole workflow.
- [Multi-select and bulk actions](flows/multi-select-bulk.md) — Let users act on sets with reversible consequences.
- [Dense data tables](flows/table-data.md) — Support scanning, sorting and keyboard manipulation of detailed data.
- [AI assistant inside app](flows/ai-assistant.md) — Make agent help transparent, controllable and falsifiable.
- [Account export and deletion](flows/account-deletion.md) — Allow accountable exit and clear control of personal data.
