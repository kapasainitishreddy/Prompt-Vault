03Three useful words

Now you know the basics.

Section

One part of a website, like its welcome area, pricing, contact details, or reviews.

Screen / flow

A screen is one view of an app. A flow is the series of steps a person takes, such as sign up → choose a goal → see progress.

Prompt

Instructions you give an AI building tool. A strong prompt says what to make, for whom, how it should behave, and what must be checked.
