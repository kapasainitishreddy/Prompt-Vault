    Research and its limits
    Sources inform the principle; the worked example and implementation order are our design synthesis. A case report is not a universal finding. Publisher content is linked, not republished.

    Papers, platform guidance, books and first-person case studies are labeled separately. There is no claim to have reviewed every publication, Reddit thread or design system.
    Start with foundations.
    Don Norman on discoverability and feedback. Steve Krug on understandable interfaces. Gould and Lewis on testing with users and iterating from evidence.
