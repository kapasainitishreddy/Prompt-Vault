Know what you are looking at

A motion film is not a website template.

These 15 examples are short finished films made through code-rendering, complete with visuals and audio. They are ideal for learning cinematic movement and creative direction.

Website effects are different: users must still be able to read, scroll, tap, type, and navigate. For smaller website interactions, visit the Motion Library or Lenis playground.
