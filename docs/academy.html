    05 / APP BUSINESS RESEARCH
    Revenue is real.
    Correlation isn't causation.
    Sensor Tower's State of Mobile 2026 identifies the top three non-game apps by 2025 worldwide in-app purchase revenue. Analyze possible product patterns without copying or claiming that UI alone produced their earnings.
    

    

    What subscription economics teach the design
    

    RevenueCat State of Subscription Apps 2026 tracks more than 115,000 subscription apps using its platform. These are observational benchmarks, not forecasts or causal experiments.
