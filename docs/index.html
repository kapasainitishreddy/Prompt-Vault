QUESTIONS / ANSWERS BEFORE THE JARGON

Wondering what
this actually does?

Simple answers for people who haven't designed or built a website before.

What is Prompt-Vault Atlas?

A free design-learning library. Explore illustrated website sections, app screens, motion examples, design principles and copyable prompts. It helps you plan and learn, but it is not a one-click app builder.

Can I really see website and app previews?

Yes. Open the Website Atlas, App Atlas or Concept Field Guide. These are original illustrative layouts rather than deployed business websites or finished native apps.

What do I do if I know absolutely nothing?

Open Start Here, select the kind of project you want, inspect the visual example, then copy a beginner-friendly prompt. Or choose Show me around on this page for an optional guided tour.

Does Atlas build or host a finished product for me?

No. Atlas provides visual references, teaching notes and prompts you can paste into a coding assistant. You still need to build, test and publish the final product yourself.

Are the motion films real?

Yes. 15 Motion Film Styles links to actual 10-second videos made by a credited open-source creator. The films are separate from illustrative website animations.
